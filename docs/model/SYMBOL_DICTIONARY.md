Notation changes are organizational unless explicitly stated otherwise.
They do not automatically alter the economic content of the model.

## Known notation collisions

The following symbols currently refer to different economic objects across the two models and require later cleanup:

- `K`: consumer memory transition kernel in the demand model vs producer search-attempt cost in the coevolutionary model.
- `e`: consumer updating effort vs producer search effort.
- `V`: consumer continuation/decision value vs producer continuation value.
- `q / Q`: objective quality in the demand model vs individual observability in the coevolutionary model.
- `C`: consumer information/updating cost vs producer production cost.
- `d`: producer search direction vs consumer choice-probability function within the coevolutionary model.

Additional notation that may require cleanup but is not yet treated as a confirmed collision:

- `x / X_t`
- `a / A / 𝒜`
- `p / P / Pr`

These are notation problems, not necessarily theoretical conflicts.

`Omega_j(d)` is not a notation collision across the two canonical models. It exists only in the coevolutionary model, where it denotes expected gain conditional on discovery.
